About Me

On the AI side, I build with LangChain, LangGraph, RAG pipelines, vector embeddings and the Gemini and OpenAI APIs. My foundation is data structures and algorithms, operating systems, DBMS and computer networks. I’m also comfortable with Python, Java, JavaScript, SQL, Docker, Git and Linux. I enjoy turning ideas into working, deployed products, and I’m looking for a team where I can keep learning and contribute from day one.
